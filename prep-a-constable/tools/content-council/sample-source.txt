Section 1 Theft Act 1968 — Basic definition of theft
A person is guilty of theft if he dishonestly appropriates property belonging to
another with the intention of permanently depriving the other of it; and "thief"
and "steal" shall be construed accordingly.

It is immaterial whether the appropriation is made with a view to gain, or is made
for the thief's own benefit.

A person guilty of theft is liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for
a term not exceeding seven years. Theft is triable either way.

Section 8 Theft Act 1968 — Robbery
A person is guilty of robbery if he steals, and immediately before or at the time
of doing so, and in order to do so, he uses force on any person or puts or seeks
to put any person in fear of being then and there subjected to force. A person
guilty of robbery is liable on conviction on indictment to imprisonment for life.
