# Constable Companion — Police Offences Compendium import

**Date:** 6 August 2026
**Source:** `Police_Offences_Compendium.docx` (supplied by Mr Mansur — "PCEP Revision
Reference, August 2026"). The document's own header says: *"Revision aid only —
always verify against current legislation (legislation.gov.uk), CPS charging
standards and force policy before operational use."*

## What was added

**60 new offence cards** were added to the Constable Companion, taking it from
**91 → 151 offences**. Two new categories were added to hold them: **Terrorism**
and **Other Operational**.

### How (cardinal-rule discipline)

- Every field (definition, points to prove, mode of trial, maximum penalty) was
  taken **verbatim from the compendium** — parsed straight from the document, not
  retyped, so there are no transcription errors and nothing is invented.
- Statute abbreviations were expanded to full names for the app's no-abbreviation
  rule (e.g. "OAPA 1861" → "Offences Against the Person Act 1861", "s.47" →
  "Section 47", "GBH" → "grievous bodily harm"). These are the same acts, spelled
  out — not content changes. The abbreviation scan over all displayed strings is
  clean.
- The **57** compendium offences that already existed in the Companion were **not
  overwritten** — the existing cards are kept as-is. Only genuinely new offences
  were added.

## The 60 new offences

**Assaults & Violence (5)** — Threats to Kill (Section 16 Offences Against the
Person Act 1861), Non-fatal Strangulation / Suffocation (Section 75A Serious Crime
Act 2015), Child Cruelty (Section 1 Children and Young Persons Act 1933),
Kidnapping / False Imprisonment (Common law), Administering Poison / Noxious Thing
(Sections 23–24 Offences Against the Person Act 1861).

**Theft & Dishonesty (3)** — Aggravated Vehicle-Taking (Section 12A Theft Act
1968), Abstracting Electricity (Section 13 Theft Act 1968), Blackmail (Section 21
Theft Act 1968).

**Fraud (3)** — Fraud (Section 1 Fraud Act 2006), Making or Supplying Articles for
Fraud (Section 7 Fraud Act 2006), Obtaining Services Dishonestly (Section 11 Fraud
Act 2006).

**Public Order (4)** — Racially / Religiously Aggravated Offences (Sections 28–32
Crime and Disorder Act 1998), Breach of the Peace (Common law), Locking On /
Being Equipped to Lock On (Sections 1–2 Public Order Act 2023), Obstruction of
Highway (Section 137 Highways Act 1980).

**Harassment & Coercion (4)** — Stalking involving Fear of Violence / Serious
Alarm or Distress (Section 4A Protection from Harassment Act 1997), False
Communications (Section 179 Online Safety Act 2023), Threatening Communications
(Section 181 Online Safety Act 2023), Improper Use of Public Electronic
Communications Network (Section 127 Communications Act 2003).

**Sexual Offences (8)** — Child Sex Offences under 13 (Sections 5–8 Sexual
Offences Act 2003), Sexual Activity with a Child (Sections 9–10), Sexual
Communication with a Child (Section 15A), Meeting a Child Following Grooming
(Section 15), Cyberflashing (Section 66A), Sharing / Threatening to Share Intimate
Images (Sections 66B–66D), Voyeurism & Upskirting (Sections 67–67A), Administering
a Substance with Intent (Section 61).

**Drugs (2)** — Obstruction (Drugs Search) (Section 23 Misuse of Drugs Act 1971),
Psychoactive Substances (Psychoactive Substances Act 2016).

**Weapons (7)** — Possession of Prohibited Weapon (Section 5 Firearms Act 1968),
Possession with Intent to Endanger Life (Section 16), Possession with Intent to
Cause Fear of Violence (Section 16A), Using Firearm to Resist Arrest (Section 17),
Carrying Firearm in a Public Place (Section 19), Threatening with Weapon / Blade
(Section 139AA Criminal Justice Act 1988 / Section 1A Prevention of Crime Act
1953), Corrosive Substance Offences (Sections 1 & 6 Offensive Weapons Act 2019).

**Driving (8)** — Causing Death by Dangerous Driving (Section 1 Road Traffic Act
1988), Causing Serious Injury by Dangerous Driving (Section 1A), Causing Death by
Careless Driving (Section 2B), Drug Driving — Specified Limits (Section 5A),
Failing to Provide a Specimen (Sections 6 & 7), Driving Whilst Disqualified
(Section 103), Failing to Stop for a Constable (Section 163), Mobile Phone Use
While Driving (Section 41D / Regulation 110).

**Police & Justice (2)** — Impersonating a Police Officer (Section 90 Police Act
1996), Escape from Lawful Custody (Common law / Section 13 Prison Act 1952).

**Terrorism (6)** — Membership of a Proscribed Organisation (Section 11 Terrorism
Act 2000), Support for a Proscribed Organisation (Sections 12–13), Possession for
Terrorist Purposes (Section 57), Collection of Information (Section 58),
Preparation of Terrorist Acts (Section 5 Terrorism Act 2006), Encouragement /
Dissemination (Sections 1–2 Terrorism Act 2006).

**Other Operational (8)** — Modern Slavery (Sections 1–2 Modern Slavery Act 2015),
Computer Misuse (Sections 1–3 Computer Misuse Act 1990), Dangerous Dogs (Section 3
Dangerous Dogs Act 1991), Aggravated Trespass (Section 68 Criminal Justice and
Public Order Act 1994), Unauthorised Encampments (Section 60C), Postal
Theft/Interference (Section 84 Postal Services Act 2000), Fireworks & Explosives
(Fireworks Act 2003 / Explosives Act 1875), **Outraging Public Decency** (Common
law — the one you asked about earlier).
