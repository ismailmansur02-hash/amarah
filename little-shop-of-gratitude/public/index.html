Mindful Paint Your Own Pottery Sessions

Enjoy your drink, pastry or afternoon tea and relax with a paint your own pottery session. Make mindful gifts and heartfelt tokens.

The 2 hour paint your own pottery session involves choosing your bisqueware and using a range of glazes to paint your ceramic piece.

After, the piece will be left to dry, then coated in transparent glaze and fired in the kiln. Once ready for collection, your pottery piece can be either picked up or posted.

How to Book

A deposit is taken upon booking and this will be redeemed against your pottery purchased on the day. To book, please email greetings@thelittleshopofgratitude.com.

Please note: any items bought at the establishment such as afternoon tea, drinks and pastries are separate and would need to be purchased directly with the vendors, and may have separate conditions which you would need to enquire about beforehand.

Cancellation Notice

48 hour policy for a full refund of the deposit.
